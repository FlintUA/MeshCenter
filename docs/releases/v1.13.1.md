# MeshCenter v1.13.1

Range: `v1.13.0..main`, PR #386 (UX2 and UX3: the Camera card, Docker without ffmpeg, repository guard).

`requirements.txt`, `adapters/meshtastic/requirements.txt`, the systemd unit, the sudoers templates, the udev rule, the privileged helper scripts, `gunicorn.conf.py`, `wsgi.py`, `install.sh` and `meshcenter-firstboot.sh` are unchanged since v1.13.0, so nothing is blocked and no manual step is needed. Open **Workspace → System → Updates**, press **Check now**, then **Update** (or **Roll back** to v1.13.0 afterwards). Reload the page with Ctrl+F5 once so the browser takes the new scripts. Docker users rebuild the image (`meshcenter:1.13.1`).

## The Camera card is one component for every camera

- **Same header for CSI, USB and network cameras**: title on the left, source picker and power button on the right, one fixed picker width. Switching sources no longer moves it.
- **One tab row** (Mode, Image, Lens) with the same icons for every type. A network camera has no Image tab, and Lens only appears when the camera reports zoom. The tabs, the image control labels (Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Sharpness, Exposure) and Defaults are now translated in all four languages (they were English in the Russian UI).
- **Status line** (resolution, frame rate, codec, bitrate, state) at the right end of the tab bar for every type; it drops under the tabs on a narrow card instead of overlapping them, and is refreshed when you switch to a local camera (it used to keep the network camera's numbers).
- **The picture box follows the video's aspect ratio** on a desktop too (no black bars above and below); on a desktop the picture runs edge to edge with square corners, and the "connecting" card has no inset frame.
- **Network camera Mode tab**: the stream picker (sub or main) and Reconnect are inside it; the facts are a tidy label and value grid (two columns, one on a phone); in a wide card (side panels hidden) the facts are compact on the left and **Take picture** with its quality and hint is on the right. The button is sized to its text, and the hint says what will really happen (the camera makes a full or small picture, the picture is cut from the running preview, or start the preview first).
- **CSI and USB controls wrap** inside the card at any width instead of being clipped on the right when both side panels are open.

## Fix: hang after switching from a network camera to a local one

Switching from a Reolink camera to USB (or CSI) a few times could leave the picture on "Connecting to camera..." until you pressed Stop and Start. The network player was not stopped when the active camera changed, so its stream and snapshot loops kept competing with the local picture. It now stops as soon as the active camera is no longer a network camera.

## Docker

- **Without ffmpeg** (the image has none yet), a camera with Reolink control shows snapshots about once a second ("Live video needs ffmpeg, showing snapshots") instead of an error; a camera without the API still gets the explanation.
- The Devices card for local cameras is one line in a container ("Local CSI/USB cameras are not available in Docker") instead of a scan button that can only find nothing.

## Network cameras card (Devices tab)

- The card spans the whole grid, row buttons wrap instead of being cut off (the Delete button was clipped), form pickers show their full texts, the form uses the standard dialog footer (equal Cancel and Save buttons), and the form carries attributes that keep password managers off the camera name and user name fields.

## For contributors

- **Repository guard**: `scripts/check_no_large_files.py` rejects files over 5 MB and `*.tar`, `*.tar.gz`, `*.tgz` (a CI step); `scripts/git-hooks/pre-commit` and `pre-push` apply it locally (`git config core.hooksPath scripts/git-hooks`, once per clone). Built Docker image archives never belong in the repository.

## Known issues

Unchanged from v1.13.0 (Docker has no ffmpeg yet; Safari before iOS 17.1 shows a picture only about once a second and only for cameras with the Reolink API; only some pages are adapted to phones; logging out cannot close a stream that is already open; Bluetooth is send-only; no remote waypoint deletion over TCP).
