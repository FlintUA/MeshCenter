# MeshCenter v1.13.10

Range: `v1.13.9..main`, two changes: a new schedule action **Send camera picture**, and a fix to its 15 minute guard found in the live check.

`requirements.txt`, `adapters/meshtastic/requirements.txt`, the systemd unit, the sudoers templates, the udev rule, the privileged helper scripts, `gunicorn.conf.py`, `wsgi.py`, `install.sh`, `meshcenter-firstboot.sh` and the Dockerfile are unchanged since v1.13.9, so nothing is blocked and no manual step is needed. There is no database change. `templates/index.html` differs from v1.13.9 only in its cache tokens. Open **Workspace > System > Updates**, press **Check now**, then **Update** (or **Roll back** to v1.13.9 afterwards). Reload the page once with Ctrl+F5 so the browser takes the new page. Docker users rebuild or load the image (`meshcenter:1.13.10`); the pinned ffmpeg version for this release is 7.1.5, unchanged.

## Schedule action: Send camera picture

- **What it does:** each run takes a fresh picture of a chosen network camera with the same Take picture service as the button (the camera's own full picture, gallery limits, sidecar), makes the send copy used by the Media gallery (1280 px by default, or 1920 px or original; location and camera data removed) and queues it to a contact with a trusted key through MCAttach, as one ordinary transfer visible in Files. The receiving side accepts it as usual.
- **Choices in the schedule form:** network camera, contact (only contacts with a secure connection are listed), resize, and the usual "when" (daily, interval or once).
- **At most one picture per 15 minutes**, in two places: an interval rule shorter than 15 minutes is refused on save (HTTP 400 `interval_too_short`, also a missing camera or contact), and at run time the rule remembers its last run (`state.last_run_at`, saved in `schedules.json`), so a restart or an edit cannot get around it. A failed attempt counts too, so a broken setup cannot retry every minute.
- **When something is not available** the run is skipped, never raised: one System Log warning and one notification with the reason (`radio_unavailable`, `key_unavailable`, `camera_unavailable`, `camera_off`, or the camera's own error such as `auth_failed`). Nothing is taken or saved to the gallery when the radio or the key is missing. The camera power switch is respected.
- **Network cameras only.** Local CSI and USB cameras are not supported by this action yet (planned as a separate change with live rounds on the USB and CSI nodes).
- **Live-verified** on dev to Flint_Echo with a 15 minute rule: three pictures queued (238,162, 224,727 and 237,189 bytes at 1280 px), all accepted and opened on the receiving node, and a run with the camera turned off was skipped with one warning and one notification and took no picture.
- Technical: `meshsrv/schedule_picture.py` (pure logic, everything injected, fake-clock tests), the transfer runs the real `POST /api/attachments` view in-process, so every validation of that route applies.

## Fixed: the 15 minute guard

- The engine ticks once a minute and compares whole seconds, so a rule that repeats every 15 minutes ran 899.x seconds after the last run about half the time and was wrongly refused ("too soon") by the run-time guard. The guard now allows 5 seconds of slack; clearly shorter gaps are still refused, and the save-time check is unchanged. Found in the live check, covered by a test.
