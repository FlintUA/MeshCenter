# MeshCenter v1.13.2

Range: `v1.13.1..main`, PRs #389 to #393 (lens zoom speed, snapshot mode, per-session sign out, login page, System page).

`requirements.txt`, `adapters/meshtastic/requirements.txt`, the systemd unit, the sudoers templates, the udev rule, the privileged helper scripts, `gunicorn.conf.py`, `wsgi.py`, `install.sh` and `meshcenter-firstboot.sh` are unchanged since v1.13.1, so nothing is blocked and no manual step is needed. Open **Workspace → System → Updates**, press **Check now**, then **Update** (or **Roll back** to v1.13.1 afterwards). Reload the page once with Ctrl+F5 so the browser takes the new scripts. Docker users rebuild the image (`meshcenter:1.13.2`).

**Existing logins stay valid after the update.** Nobody has to sign in again: a login made before this version keeps working and simply gets a session id on its next request, so it can be signed out individually from then on.

## Sign out, and Sign out everywhere

- **Sign out** (Security card on the System page) now ends only the session of the browser you press it in. Other browsers and phones stay signed in, a copy of that browser's cookie stops working, and a camera stream that session had open ends within a few seconds. The revoked sessions are remembered across a restart in `data/revoked_sessions.json` (a small file, entries expire with the login cookie).
- **Sign out everywhere** (new button next to it, with a confirmation) ends every session on every device, this one included, and every open camera stream. Use it when a phone or a shared computer may still be signed in. A password change still ends every other session, as before.
- A page whose session was ended from another tab or device now goes to the login page by itself (it used to stay open with a blank picture and stale numbers).
- The 10 minute limit of a camera stream stays as the last resort.
- **Forgot the password?** The password is stored only as a hash, but it can be reset from the device: README (Security) and User Guide 18 show the three-line command (it also ends every old session) and the Docker variant. You then choose a new password in the setup wizard.
- The login page counts the wait after wrong passwords down and unlocks **Sign in** when it is over (the number used to stay frozen).

## Lens zoom: faster and more responsive

A newer zoom target no longer waits for the older move to be read back: the older wait ends at once and the last target gets the final read-back. The camera is not blocked while the lens moves (a snapshot or a read can go in between), and the client keeps one connection and the login token alive instead of a new TLS handshake per request. Measured on the Reolink RLC-811A on a LAN: a move takes about 0.5 to 0.7 s end to end, with no new login and no new connection per call, and the last of three quick drags wins.

## Snapshot mode (no MediaSource, or no ffmpeg in Docker)

- The status line and the Mode tab show the picture's own size and the refresh (1 per second); codec, frame rate and bitrate are hidden for pictures.
- The label over the picture is gone (it covered the camera's own on-screen text); the status line says "Snapshots".
- The stream picker (sub or main) is locked in snapshot mode with a one-line reason.
- The status line has no leading "-", and the dock hides "Uptime --" until the radio reports an uptime.

## Fixes

- **System page**: with both side panels open the right column (System Log, Radio Health, Security) was cut off and could not be reached; the two columns now stack when the workspace is narrow and stay side by side when there is room.

## Known issues

- On a phone the Security card cannot be reached, so Sign out and the password cannot be changed there (use another device). Phone layout of Map, System, Settings, About, Node Manager and Chats is the next batch (v1.13.3).
- Docker: no network camera picture until the image gets ffmpeg (a camera with Reolink control shows snapshots instead).
- Unchanged from v1.13.1: Safari before iOS 17.1 shows a picture only about once a second and only for cameras with the Reolink API; Bluetooth is send-only; no remote waypoint deletion over TCP.
