# MeshCenter v1.13.6

Range: `v1.13.5..main`, one change: the official Telegram channel and the community chat are linked from the About page and the README.

`requirements.txt`, `adapters/meshtastic/requirements.txt`, the systemd unit, the sudoers templates, the udev rule, the privileged helper scripts, `gunicorn.conf.py`, `wsgi.py`, `install.sh` and `meshcenter-firstboot.sh` are unchanged since v1.13.5, so nothing is blocked and no manual step is needed. Open **Workspace → System → Updates**, press **Check now**, then **Update** (or **Roll back** to v1.13.5 afterwards). Reload the page once with Ctrl+F5 so the browser takes the new page. Docker users rebuild the image (`meshcenter:1.13.6`).

## Telegram channel and community chat

- **About page:** the Overview tab lists both links next to the creator's contacts, and the Links tab has two new cards, each with a short description in all four languages (English, German, Russian, Ukrainian).
  - **Telegram Channel:** [t.me/meshcenter_official](https://t.me/meshcenter_official) for official news, releases and development updates.
  - **Community Chat:** [t.me/meshcenter_community](https://t.me/meshcenter_community) to ask questions, share ideas and discuss MeshCenter with other users.
- **README:** a Telegram badge at the top and a new **Community & Updates** section with the same two links (community languages: English, German, Ukrainian and Russian).

Nothing else changed: the page differs from v1.13.5 by these four links and the cache-busting version number.

## Known issues

Unchanged from v1.13.5 (see its notes).
